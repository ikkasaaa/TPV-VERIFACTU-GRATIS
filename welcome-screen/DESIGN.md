# Diseño de la pantalla de bienvenida

Dirección elegida: **Cuaderno de pegatinas cósmico**. Salió de un panel de tres conceptos independientes (cuaderno de pegatinas, observatorio de cristal, agencia espacial retro) puntuados por tres jueces (cliente, dirección de arte, ingeniería) y luego sintetizados en una única especificación de construcción.

## Tesis

For a bar owner the scary word is "Hacienda"; the app's promise is that the tax part becomes a sticker you no longer think about. The welcome screen is a sticker-album page laid over a candy night sky, and the one sticker that matters is the ticket-with-QR seal: tap "Empezar" and it peels off the sky and lands on your first real ticket. Everything else (copy, ticket, form, glass) stays sober and true to a POS.

## Tipografía

- **Display:** Baloo 2 (800 only). Chunky rounded sticker lettering without being a novelty face; handles ñ and accents. Used ONLY for the three slide titles, the two screen titles ('Tu negocio', 'Entrar'), the € glyph inside the coin sticker, the tape label and the two primary CTA labels. Dropping 700 saves a file: the tape label uses 800 at 13pt.
- **Texto:** Figtree (400 body, 500 links/labels/captions, 600 field labels, chip text, status-bar clock). Friendly geometric sans with a large x-height that stays legible at 15pt over blurred nebula; its plainness keeps the tax copy sober next to Baloo 2.
- **Utilidad:** Courier Prime (400 only). The sample ticket, ticket number and QR caption are set in a thermal-receipt monospace. It is the one place the eye must say 'this is a real receipt'. 700 is cut; emphasis on the ticket is uppercase, not bold. The 'VERI*FACTU' micro-caption inside the seal SVG is drawn as paths, not text.
- **Escala:** Slide title 34/38pt Baloo 2 800, letter-spacing -0.01em, max 2 lines (titles are 3-4 words). Screen title 34/38 Baloo 2 800. Subtitle (visually hidden h1 companion, used on screen 2 intro) 17/24 Figtree 500. Body 15/22 Figtree 400, capped at 3 lines (~130 chars). Label/caption 13/18 Figtree 500; uppercase only for the status bar and the tape label (tracking +0.06em, Baloo 2 800 13pt for the tape). Field label 13/18 Figtree 600. Input text 17/24 Figtree 400. Chip 15/20 Figtree 600. Button 17/20 Baloo 2 800 (primary), 15/20 Figtree 500 (secondary links). Ticket 12/15 Courier Prime 400; ticket caption 11/14. Helper/validation 13/18 Figtree 500. Dynamic Island label 12/14 Figtree 500.

## Elemento firma: El sello

The ticket-with-QR sticker: a die-cut of a small thermal receipt (Nata paper 64x82 inside a 96pt-tall sticker, zig-zag torn bottom edge with 7 teeth, three Uva 55% print lines, a 30x30 Menta QR block with three Tinta finder squares, a 'VERI*FACTU' caption drawn as tiny paths, a Chicle 12% band down the right third, a Nata 70% oval highlight top-left of the QR, and a top-right corner drawn already lifted with a 6pt Nata underside triangle). It is the only sticker drawn upright, the only one that does not float, and the only element in the design allowed to be theatrical. It is also the only sticker whose look is repeated: on screen 2 the same SVG sits at 56pt on the top-right corner of the live sample ticket as the VeriFactu seal, with a Menta tick and 'Ticket verificable'.

**Cómo se mueve.** Idle: breathes scale 1→1.03→1 over 4s ease-in-out; every 7s (offset 2s) the lifted corner flicks +2° for 300ms (a 'ready to peel' hint); on slide 2 it is nudged (scale 1.15, 20pt toward the page) and a short chain of two tiny ticket dots draws a line to it. On 'Empezar': 120-380ms the top-right half peels (a clipped flap rotates -150° around the top-left→bottom-right diagonal through a 600px perspective, revealing a Nata underside), 380ms the whole seal lifts to scale 1.12 with the shadow layer sliding down 8pt and gaining opacity; 380-900ms it flies on a shallow arc (a translate path through one control point 60pt above the straight line, keyframed by JS with a spring ease) to the landing slot on the screen-2 sample ticket, scaling to .58; on landing it squashes (scaleX 1.08 / scaleY .92 → 1, 180ms), its QR modules print in row by row over 300ms (5 row groups, opacity 0→1, 60ms stagger) and the Menta tick + caption fade in. Back reverses it exactly. Reduced motion: no peel, no flight; the seal cross-fades between its two homes in 200ms.

**Por qué encaja con el producto.** VeriFactu's visible artefact is precisely this: a QR every ticket must carry, hashed and chained. Making that the sticker the owner 'puts on' their first ticket turns the compliance requirement into the one satisfying gesture of the screen and explains the product without a paragraph. It also carries the sober Courier Prime receipt onto screen 1, so the trust anchor is there before the form.

Receta común: One recipe so all seven read as one sheet. (1) DOM per sticker: <div class=\"sticker\" data-id> → <div class=\"st-anchor\"> (JS-owned translate: home position, drag, settle; nothing else animates here) → <div class=\"st-float\"> (CSS keyframe loops that NEVER restart: float, rotate, breathe, twinkle) → <div class=\"st-pose\"> (short reactions: pop-in, tap bounce, nudge, lift) → two siblings: <div class=\"st-shadow\"><svg><use href=\"#sil-id\"/></svg></div> and <div class=\"st-art\"><svg>…</svg></div>. Because drag lives on .st-anchor and loops on .st-float, a loop continues seamlessly from the drop position; pop-in and tap live on .st-pose so they never fight the loop. (2) Die-cut: each SVG declares its silhouette once as an explicit path <path id=\"sil-id\"> (the union of all shapes, hand-drawn, including flame/ring/teeth). The art paints, in order: <use href=\"#sil-id\"> filled Nata with stroke Nata width 8 round joins (renders as the 4pt outer die-cut), then the coloured shapes, then a 1.5pt Uva 60% inner marker line on the main silhouettes only, then a 1pt Nata 40% inner rim inset 2pt on the main form (thickness). No filters, no gradients, no <text> inside stickers. (3) Cell shading: at most two candy colours + Nata per sticker; one darker band (the 'sombra' token) lower-right, one Nata 70-75% oval highlight upper-left; light always top-left. Menta only on the seal QR and the comet edge. (4) Shadow: the .st-shadow layer paints the silhouette in rgba(20,16,40,.35) at translate(0,6); it is a separate composited element, so 'lift' = .st-shadow → translate(0,14) scale(1.04) opacity .45 and .st-art → translateY(-6) scale(1.08), both transform/opacity, 180ms; no filter anywhere on stickers. (5) Sizes 56-120pt; the seal is 96pt and upright; all others rest between -14° and +28° so the sky looks stuck on by hand. (6) All colours via CSS variables (--nata, --chicle, …) referenced with fill=\"var(--chicle)\" so the sheet re-tints together (the CTA swap token is separate). (7) Hit area: the .st-art svg has pointer-events: visiblePainted; minimum touch target 56pt (pad the SVG viewBox if the art is smaller). (8) Stickers are aria-hidden and outside the tab order; they are pure delight.

## Cristal (glassmorphism)

Album page (screen 1) and form panel (screen 2), class .glass: position absolute; border-radius 28pt; background: linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(23,32,61,.50) 0%, rgba(23,32,61,.62) 100%) over a base layer rgba(255,246,234,.08) (declared as two backgrounds, Tinta gradient on top); backdrop-filter: blur(22px) saturate(150%); -webkit-backdrop-filter same; border: 1px solid rgba(255,246,234,.30); box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.42), inset 0 -1px 0 rgba(23,32,61,.35), 0 24px 56px rgba(10,8,30,.48); isolation: isolate; contain: paint; transform: translateZ(0); overflow: hidden. Measured worst case (Chicle blob at 38% directly behind, tint 50%): Nata body text ≈ 9.5:1; at tint 62% ≈ 10.5:1; the build must verify by sampling a screenshot pixel under the body copy (target ≥ 7:1, hard floor 4.5:1). Nebula blob centres are constrained outside the page's bounding box (Chicle centre at (220,140), Albaricoque at (400,330), Uva at (60,760) but at 45% of a dark colour it only deepens the tint). Tape label: background rgba(255,176,103,.85), 1px dashed rgba(255,246,234,.40) edge, radius 8pt, rotate(-2deg), no blur, Tinta text (8.9:1); it is a graphic, not a panel. Back button (screen 2): 44pt circle, same .glass recipe with blur(16px) (second and last live blur on screen 2). Fields (inside the panel, no blur of their own): background rgba(255,246,234,.10); border 1px rgba(255,246,234,.28); inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.35); radius 16pt; focus: background rgba(255,246,234,.16), outline 3px solid var(--menta), outline-offset 3px. Chips: same as fields at radius 999; selected: background var(--chicle), color var(--tinta) (6.6:1), border transparent. Login sheet: .glass with blur(22px), top radius 32pt, sits over a scrim rgba(34,48,90,.35); while it is open the album page gets .blur-off (backdrop-filter none; background rgba(23,32,61,.72)) so only one blur is live. CTA sticker: fill var(--cta-fill) (Chicle), label var(--tinta) Baloo 2 800 (6.6:1; Albaricoque swap gives 8.9:1); Nata 4pt outline, Uva 60% 1.5pt inner line, in-SVG silhouette shadow like every sticker. Fallback: @supports not (backdrop-filter: blur(1px)) and not (-webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(1px)) → .glass { background: rgba(23,32,61,.88); } and the astronaut/parked stickers behind it are simply hidden by it. Blur budget: at most two backdrop-filter surfaces live at any time (screen 1: page; screen 2: panel + back button; sheet open: sheet only).

## Ambiente

Six layers behind the stickers, back to front, all transform/opacity. (1) Ground: linear-gradient(180deg, #17203D 0%, #1B2648 45%, #22305A 100%) so the darker ink sits behind the busy sticker zone and the lifted petrol behind the text; no vignette (it pushed the ground toward black). Page backdrop outside the phone: #121A33 with a radial Albaricoque 8% glow behind the frame. (2) Nebula depth: one Uva radial-gradient div 520pt at 45%, centre (60,760), translate loop ±30pt over 70s. (3) Nebula candy: Chicle blob 460pt at 38% centre (220,140) and Albaricoque blob 380pt at 38% centre (400,330), loops of 48s and 58s in opposite directions with scale .96→1.04; both follow pointer/gyro parallax at 0.5x; both are always fully opaque at their target so the ground never reads as black. (4) Star field: three static inline SVGs (120 far dots 0.5-1pt, 60 mid 1.5pt, 25 near 2.5pt, all Nata at 60/80/100%) each transformed as one group for parallax 0.3x/0.7x/1.2x (max ±6/±10/±14pt, lerped 0.08 per frame). Twinkle: 14 separate absolutely positioned 2pt divs (not inside the SVGs) with their own opacity keyframes .4→1 at 2.1s/3.3s/4.7s; halved to 7 and near stars to 12 when navigator.deviceMemory ≤ 4 or connection.saveData. (5) Shooting stars: one 90x1.5pt Nata→transparent gradient div rotated -35°, translate-only 320pt crossing in 700ms, spawned every 4-7s at a random x within the band y 70-330 only (never over the page or the tape), top-right to bottom-left, one at a time. (6) Slide-2 chain overlay: a small SVG that draws a Nata 55% 2pt line from (70,300) toward the seal's current anchor with two 6pt Nata ticket dots on it; revealed with scaleX 0→1 along the line and dots popping; hidden on other slides. Parallax source: pointermove on fine pointers; deviceorientation on phones, requested only after the first tap on iOS (DeviceOrientationEvent.requestPermission inside a user gesture), never on load. Idle cadence so the sky is never still for more than ~4s: rocket flame continuous, twinkle continuous, shooting star 4-7s, coin flip hint 8s (offset 4s), seal corner flick 7s (offset 2s), comet 7-10s, rocket thrust 11s, astronaut wave 12s. Reduced motion: layers 2-4 present and frozen, twinkle off, shooting stars off, parallax off, comet parked.

## Riesgos asumidos

- Playfulness vs trust: mitigated by sober, specific copy (AEAT, NIF, Ticket n.º), a credible Courier Prime receipt on both screens, no faces on stickers, and the only theatrical element being the compliance artefact. Escape hatch kept: the CTA colour is one token (--cta-fill) and stickers can be reduced to seal + coin + planet without touching the system.
- Contrast is asserted from a colour-math worst case (≥ 9:1 at 50% tint over a 38% Chicle blob); the engineer must sample the built page (screenshot pixel under body copy on slides 1-3 with the Chicle blob at its nearest loop position) and raise the tint gradient toward .58/.70 if any sample drops under 7:1.
- 3D peel on iOS Safari: perspective + clip-path + preserve-3d inside a transformed, clipped .screen can flicker. The peel is isolated in its own #seal-layer wrapper (no ancestor with transform-style other than the flap container) and gated by PEEL_3D; the fallback (corner flick + lift) keeps the transition complete. Test on a real iPhone before shipping.
- FLIP landing accuracy depends on measuring the slot in the final layout; measuring while the panel is mid-animation gives an offset landing. The .measure step must run with transitions disabled and before the timeline starts, and the handoff must happen only after the landing keyframe ends.
- Real-phone keyboard: the on-screen keyboard shrinks 100dvh; the form panel is the only scrolling element (overflow-y auto, overscroll-behavior contain) and the parked stickers have pointer-events: none on screen 2, so taps around fields never lift a sticker.
- Gesture collision budget: drags start only on .st-art inside the sky; the pager only listens inside the page; the sheet's swipe-down only on its handle/header; the comet only accepts click. Any pointerdown on a sticker calls stopPropagation before the pager can see it.
- File size: Baloo 2 800, Figtree 400/500/600, Courier Prime 400, latin subset, come to ~110-140KB base64; seven SVGs and three star SVGs add ~40KB. Keep under 400KB total; do not add a font weight.
- Slide 3 offline claim was removed pending confirmation from the owner; the previous line ('Funciona sin conexión y envía los tickets pendientes cuando vuelve la red.') is kept in a comment as an optional swap if the product does this.
- Ground reading as black on dim phones: the gradient lifts to #22305A at the bottom and the two candy blobs are always at 38%; the engineer should check the built screen next to a #000 swatch and, if it still reads black, raise the top stop to #1A2444.
